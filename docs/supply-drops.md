# Supply Drops

A chest of gold lands somewhere in the world every so often. First come, first served.

```
/drop check
```

Shows every supply drop currently sitting out there uncollected, with coordinates. If nothing shows
up, either none are out right now or they've all been grabbed.

## What's in one

Always a solid amount of gold, split randomly across ingots, blocks, nuggets, and raw gold. Worth
the trip. There's also a chance of a bonus rare item on top: a Mending book, netherite, emeralds, a
totem of undying, or a shulker box.

A bonus item is always usable regardless of your skill mastery level, even if you haven't unlocked
that item's normal luxury gate yet.

## Getting there first

Once a drop is announced, it's a race. Whoever opens the chest and takes the loot keeps it, no
protection or claim involved. Up to 5 drops can exist in the world unclaimed at once.
