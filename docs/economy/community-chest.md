# Community Chest

`/nation chest` opens a shared 54-slot (double-chest-sized) storage space for your nation. Any
member can open it, deposit into it, or take from it — no permission needed. It's meant for casual
sharing: spare tools, building supplies, or anything the nation wants pooled together.

It's also the easiest way to hand off a **[mastery-gated luxury item](../skills.md#luxury-gating-why-cant-i-get-that-item)**
(Netherite, a Shulker Box, an Enchanted Golden Apple, a Totem, Mending gear, a Trident, or a
Strength II Potion) to a nation-mate who hasn't mastered that skill themselves — drop it in the
chest instead of arranging a meetup.

## Restrictions

- **Locked during an active battle.** You can still use it any other time your nation is at war
  (declared, queued, awaiting approval) — it only locks once the fight actually starts.
- **Blocked while you're combat-tagged** (recently in PvP) — you can't duck into it mid-fight.
- **No gold currency.** Gold ingots, nuggets, blocks, and raw gold can't be placed in the chest —
  including hidden inside a shulker box or bundle. This matches the same rule that keeps gold out
  of containers on unclaimed land (see [World Rules](../world-rules.md)).
